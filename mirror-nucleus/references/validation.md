# Validation ladder

| Rung | Check | How | Proves | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Tools | `scripts/selftest.py` PASS (needs cluster-potential) | mirror construction, spectrum prediction, ANC ratio, arithmetic | your mirror pair |
| 1 | Transfer | the mirror's bound states predicted with nothing refitted, against evaluated levels | the potential is charge symmetric as set up | widths |
| 2 | Geometry | ANC ratio and θ² ratio over ≥ 5 geometries / radii; quote the spread | the ratio is a model-independent statement | — |
| 3 | Fair refit | free and constrained variants on one level structure, same data, same penalties; Δχ² and per-dataset costs | the cost of charge symmetry for those channels | channels the mirror data do not see |
| 4 | Coverage | list the reaction channels in each project; a channel without data in the mirror is untested | what the test can and cannot say | — |

## Lessons that changed results (7Be/7Li study)

- **States are mirrors; the α widths are not.** The 7Be/7Li negative-parity resonances agreed in θ² to 8–28 %. The 1/2⁺ α width of 7Li came out at a third to a half of the 7Be value. Imposing the 7Be widths on the 7Li analogs, with energies free, cost Δχ² = +1.07 × 10³, mostly in the t+α elastic data, and pushed the 3/2⁺ out of the data range. The large α width encodes the s-wave continuum (the scattering length) of each system, which differs between the mirrors.
- **The 7Li project had no n+6Li data**, so the nucleon-width half of the charge-symmetry prediction could not be tested. Adding 6Li(n,t)α and n+6Li elastic data is the next step.
- **The ANC ratio exposed an inconsistency** the individual fits hid: 2.28 between the evaluations against 1.333 ± 0.008 from the cluster model.
- **The radius mismatch was not the cause.** The 1/2⁺ θ² ratio was 2.1 at every common radius from 4.2 to 6.5 fm.
